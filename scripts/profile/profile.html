01 · About us

Italian know-how,
Egyptian manufacturing.

EGYTALTEX is a well-established Egyptian–Italian garment manufacturer with more than 25 years of experience in textile and apparel production.

Operating from a fully equipped facility in Cairo, we specialise in high-quality knitted garments for export markets. We serve clients in Europe, the United States and Canada, supplying reliable, consistent and compliant production tailored to international standards.

Our strength lies in combining Italian production know-how with competitive manufacturing in Egypt — ensuring quality, flexibility and cost efficiency.
